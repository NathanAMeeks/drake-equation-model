# v7 candidate variables: geology, biology, astronomy (research only, 2026-10-30)

These are proposals for the radio-or-better (now) model. **Nothing has been implemented.** Each one has MEASURED or MODELED data that could set a range. Excluded: anything already in `params.yaml` (plate tectonics, land+ocean, large moon, Jupiter shield, C/O, rocky composition, K activity, M flares/water/habitability, F UV, GHZ/migration, metallicity, binaries, SN/GRB, Big-Five, oxygen, recovery, hard steps, stage hazards as priors), and anything about radio leakage or detectability.

Ranked by data quality x likely impact on the headline. "Overlap" means it would refine an existing factor instead of adding a new one, so it must not double-count.

| # | candidate (field) | how it enters | data / source | data-backed range | direction | tag |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Climate-stability window for agriculture** (geology/biology) | Gate on the lithic->agricultural advance. Agriculture can start only during long stable interglacials. The share of time in interglacial mode, with episode lengths of 10-30 kyr, scales the effective advance rate. | PAGES Past Interglacials WG 2016, Rev. Geophys., https://doi.org/10.1002/2015rg000482 (11 interglacials in 800 kyr, 10-30 kyr each). IPCC AR4 6.4: about 20% of each cycle is interglacial, https://archive.ipcc.ch/publications_and_data/ar4/wg1/en/ch6s6-4.html. Mechanism: Richerson, Boyd & Bettinger 2001, Am. Antiquity 66:387, https://doi.org/10.2307/2694241 | interglacial fraction about 0.2 (Earth, last 800 kyr); stable spells 1e4-3e4 yr | down (longer lithic stage). It may partly overlap the Bayes-updated stage_tau_agri, which is already timed on Earth. | MEASURED (Earth record) |
| 2 | **Airless rocky planets around M dwarfs** (astronomy) | Lowers the M-class habitable fraction for HZ rocky planets: the share keeping an atmosphere. Overlaps f_m_habitable / f_m_flares, so it should replace their [GUESS] part, not multiply on top. | JWST: TRAPPIST-1 b bare rock, Greene+2023 Nature, https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-05951-7. TRAPPIST-1 c no thick CO2 atmosphere, Zieba+2023 Nature, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10447244/ | So far a handful of hot inner planets; none show a thick atmosphere. The range would come from the JWST rocky-worlds programme as it grows. | down (M share). The headline is K-dominated (81%), so the effect is moderate. | MEASURED (small N) |
| 3 | **Superflare rate on Sun-like stars** (astronomy) | Floor on the industrial/radio collapse hazards (grid- and satellite-scale damage) for G/K hosts: rate x P(collapse or regress per event). | Vasilyev+2024 Science 386:1301, https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adl5441: flares above 1e34 erg occur (8.63 +/- 0.20)e-3 per star per yr, power-law index 1.97 +/- 0.30 | rate 8.4e-3 to 8.8e-3 /yr (above 1e34 erg). P(regress) per event needs a MODELED damage curve (none chosen yet). | down (shorter industrial/radio stages); could be large if P(regress) is not small | rate MEASURED; damage MODELED |
| 4 | **Km-scale asteroid impacts on technological civilizations** (astronomy/geology) | Size-resolved hazard on agricultural+ stages, below the Big-Five scale: at least 1 km gives global effects and partial disruption, at least 10 km gives civilization-ending effects. | Harris & Chodas 2021 Icarus 365:114452, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.icarus.2021.114452. Table in Harris (DLR reprint), https://elib.dlr.de/100120/1/3004_Harris_reprint.pdf | at least 1 km: about 1 per 5e5 yr; at least 10 km: about 1 per 1e8 yr (Earth) | down, small (5e5 yr is long compared with tech stages) | MEASURED |
| 5 | **Super-eruptions (M8)** (geology) | Regress hazard on agricultural/industrial stages. | Rougier+2018 EPSL, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.epsl.2017.11.015: global M8 return period 17 kyr (95% CI 5.2-48 kyr) | 1/48 kyr to 1/5.2 kyr | down. The regress probability per event is the open part; humanity survived Toba. | MEASURED (rate) |
| 6 | **Extreme pandemics** (biology) | Regress hazard on agricultural+ stages, from the measured intensity-frequency of novel epidemics. | Marani+2021 PNAS, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2105482118, plus the 2023 correction, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2302169120: Spanish-flu intensity about 0.114%/yr (recurrence about 879 yr, 95% CI 730-1081) | as stated | down, small (these events did not cause regression on Earth) | MEASURED (Earth, last 400 yr) |
| 7 | **Radiogenic heat budget (Th)** (geology/astronomy) | Modulates how long plate tectonics or a mantle carbon cycle lasts. It would set the spread of the existing f_plate_tectonics / habitable lifetime (overlap). | Unterborn+2015 ApJ 806:139, https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-637x/806/1/139: stellar Th 59%-251% of solar in solar twins/analogues | 0.59-2.51 x solar heat budget from Th | up or down, broadening. Needs a MODELED map from heat budget to tectonic lifetime. | MEASURED (stellar Th) |
| 8 | **Water-world fraction for M-dwarf small planets** (astronomy) | Splits the M-class rocky HZ planets into rocky versus water-rich (no exposed land, so the land path is closed and only the ocean path remains). Refines f_land_and_ocean per class (overlap). | Luque & Palle 2022 Science, https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abl7164: a density gap separates rocky and water-rich populations | fraction from their sample (to extract from the paper's table) | down for the land path on M dwarfs; the ocean path is unchanged | MEASURED |
| 9 | **Escape barrier on super-Earths** (astronomy) | Gate on radio->spacefaring for hosts above about 10 Earth masses (chemical rockets infeasible), using the measured rocky-planet mass distribution. | Hippke 2018/2019 IJA 18:393, https://doi.org/10.1017/s1473550418000198 | chemical launch feasible up to about 10 Earth masses (Isp 350-450 s) | Moves civilizations from spacefaring to radio. Little change to radio-or-better; it only matters for the v6 probe side analysis. | MODELED |
| 10 | **Encounters with cold dense interstellar clouds** (astronomy) | Episodic hazard or forcing: the heliosphere shrinks inside 1 AU, and the climate effect is uncertain. | Opher, Loeb & Peek 2024 Nature Astronomy, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41550-024-02279-8: a possible encounter 2-3 Myr ago, probability at least 1.3% | rate about 1 per few Myr per star; effect on life not quantified | unclear, probably small | MODELED |

**Notes.**
- Items 3-6 all sit on the existing stage hazards (stage_h_*), which are currently broad loguniform priors. If added, they would set measured lower bounds on those hazards rather than new free factors.
- Earth-only data (items 1, 4, 5, 6) are one sample. Ranges describe Earth's variability, not the spread across planets.
- Still to do before implementing: pull exact numbers from the Luque & Palle tables (item 8), choose a MODELED damage curve (item 3), and choose a heat-to-tectonics map (item 7).

# Odds-raising candidates (research only, 2026-10-31)

These are the counterweight to the list above: data-backed factors that would potentially **raise** the radio-or-better count. **Nothing has been implemented.** Excluded: what is already in `params.yaml` as its own factor (superhabitable boost, ocean path, exomoons, multi-moon, cross-lineage tool use) and radio leakage. Most items refine an existing factor, so the "overlap" column says which one and how.

Ranked by data quality x likely impact.

| # | candidate (field) | how it enters | source (verified) | data-backed range | direction | tag | overlap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Faster ecological recovery after a mass extinction** (biology/geology) | Shorten the post-extinction recovery delay (v5 currently fixes it at 10 Myr, Kirchner & Weil 2000). | Lyson+2019 Science, https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aay2268: after the K-Pg extinction, mammal richness doubled and maximum body mass was near pre-extinction levels within ~100 kyr; a threefold increase in maximum mass by ~300 kyr; more large mammals by ~700 kyr | 0.1-0.7 Myr for ecological (body-size) recovery, against 10 Myr for global diversity | up. In v5, recovery cost about -0.17 dex in the headline median; this would give back part of that. | MEASURED (one basin, Colorado) | replaces or widens `t_recovery_gyr` (the time to recover tool-user-capable niches is between the two numbers) |
| 2 | **Agriculture arose independently many times** (biology) | Evidence that the lithic-to-agricultural step is fast once climate allows it: narrows `stage_tau_agri_yr` (prior 3.3e5-3.3e7 yr) toward the low end. | Larson+2014 PNAS, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1323964111: at least 11 independent centres of domestication | at least 11 independent origins, all in the Holocene | up (shorter lithic stage) | MEASURED | `stage_tau_agri_yr`. **Checked and not proposed:**
- Longer M-dwarf habitable windows: already `th_m_gyr` 10-50 Gyr.
- Tidal heating extending habitable lifetimes: I found no paper with a data-backed range this turn.
- Earlier oxygenation on other worlds: no measured exoplanet data; the oxygen lag is already a measured Earth anchor.
- Re-emergence of intelligence after collapse: already `m_recurrence`.

**Balance note.** The strongest data-backed raisers (#1-#5) mostly sit on existing factors whose current ranges are conservative: fixed 10 Myr recovery, broad agricultural-step and regress priors, `ne_gk` capped at 0.88. Changing them would be range updates rather than new free factors.
